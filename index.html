The risk hiding in old clock-in systems

Most systems assume stable power and constant signal — conditions that can't be guaranteed at every location.

Risk 01

Single point of failure

GPS-only or app-only clock-in tools go dark during power outages or in low-signal locations — exactly when accurate records matter most.

Risk 02

Wage compliance exposure

Manual rate calculations for weekends and holidays are a recurring source of underpayment disputes and compliance risk.

Risk 03

No per-site flexibility

A multi-branch chain doesn't have identical sites. Fixed verification methods force a worst-case setup everywhere.

Configured per site

Three verification modes, one dashboard

Each location runs the method that fits its conditions — switched centrally, not hardcoded into the app.

WiFi / IP match

Confirms the device is on the store's network. No extra hardware, works indoors, and caches for brief outages.

Fit: fixed sites with in-store WiFi

GPS radius

Confirms the device is within a defined distance of the site. Suited to outdoor seating and semi-permanent locations.

Fit: open-air venues, markets, pop-ups

QR code

A posted code staff scan on arrival. Zero network dependency — the fallback that keeps working through outages.

Fit: backup mode, low-connectivity sites
